# Security Audit — `record_shop`

Audited at `e3fbb48237cbc131f8a66d332409a8cc1093954f` (2026-10-21). Covers `record_shop::shop` and `record_shop::witness`, the privacy fixtures under `fixtures/privacy/`, and the money path through the pinned `release_license::forward` (misofm/framework `54c88cd`), `royalty_pool::pool::derive_address` and `miso_music::release` (`f404d77`). Later commits apply the Sui section order and repin the dependencies. The commit "Forward sales through release_license" (`d498682`, 2026-10-24) then replaced the equal-split `release_revenue_forwarder` with `release_license::forward` (basis-point shares, rounding remainder to the Release), and made `set_pricing` take the `Release` and check it (`EWrongRelease`, `ENoSplit`). Every statement below was re-checked against that code. Line references are to the current code.

## What it does

Fixed- or floor-price sales of Records. The only state is a `Pricing` dynamic field per currency on each Pressing at `PricingKey<Currency>()`. `purchase` validates the payment against that pricing, presses the Record through the package witness, and forwards the whole payment to the royalty-pool addresses of the Release's tracks in proportion to the Release's basis-point split. See [README](./README.md) and [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](./docs/ARCHITECTURE.md).

## Trust model

- **Pressing admin** (`PressingAdminCap`): `set_pricing` and `remove_pricing` go through `pressing::uid_mut(cap)` (`shop.move:101,111`), so the cap check is `record`'s. `set_pricing` additionally requires the Pressing's own Release and a split on it (`shop.move:99-100`). The same cap authorizes or revokes `record_shop::witness::Witness` on the Pressing. Cannot touch proceeds: none are held.
- **Buyer** (anyone): `purchase<Currency>(&mut Pressing, &Release, Balance<Currency>, &Clock)` (`shop.move:52-67`). Chooses only the currency and amount; the pricing, the Record number, the `Currency` provenance and the destination of funds are fixed by state.
- **Release admin** (`ReleaseAdminCap`): sets and replaces the split with `release_license::set_split` (`release_license.move:121-139`) and receives the rounding remainder at the Release address, which it redeems with `release_license::redeem_and_forward` (`release_license.move:172-179`). `record::pressing::new` requires this cap (`pressing.move:153`), so it is also the initial Pressing admin.
- **Recording admins**: receive proceeds in their recordings' `Currency` pools; they alone can create those pools (`pool::new_for_recording` needs `RecordingAdminCap`, `pool.move:228-233`). Their `release_license::authorize` records acceptance of a split hash but `forward` never reads it (`release_license.move:71-96`): payment does not wait on authorization.
- **Nobody** outside this package can construct `Witness` (`witness.move:8,12-14`; both fixtures fail to compile with the expected diagnostics) or `PricingKey` (module-private constructor; probe failed with `EC04001`). No function of this package holds, stores or returns funds.

## Findings

**RS-01 · Low · Accepted — Proceeds for a currency without a pool are stranded until the pool exists.** `forward` sends each track's share to `pool::derive_address<Currency>(recording_id)` (`release_license.move:81`) whether or not a `RoyaltyPool<_, Currency>` has been created there. `set_pricing<Currency>` performs no such check (`shop.move:93-108`; it checks only the Release and its split), so a Pressing admin can sell in a currency for which some recording has no pool; those funds sit at the derived address until that recording's admin calls `new_for_recording<_, Currency>` and the pool redeems them. Documented in SECURITY.md; by design, setup order is left to tooling.

**RS-02 · Low · Open — Pool addresses are bound to the royalty-pool package that `release_license` links.** `derive_address` hashes `RoyaltyPoolKey<Currency>` including its defining package address (`pool.move:222-224`). `Move.lock` pins `royalty_pool` at framework `54c88cd` through `release_license`; if the pools that tooling creates on mainnet come from a different publication of `royalty_pool`, every sale's proceeds go to addresses no object can ever claim, with no abort. Suggested fix: at publish time, before authorizing the Witness on any Pressing, verify that the linked `pool::derive_address<Currency>(recording_id)` equals the ID of an existing shared pool; keep the `royalty_pool` pin identical across `release_license`, the pool-creation tooling and this package.

**RS-03 · Info · Accepted — A payment too small for any share to round up pays only the Release.** Each track receives `shares[i].apply(paid)`, rounded down, and a track whose value is 0 is skipped (`release_license.move:77-84`). The remainder, less than one unit per track with a nonzero share, goes to the Release address; when every share rounds to 0, no `ReleaseRevenueForwardedEvent` is emitted (`remainder < total_value` is false, `release_license.move:86-93`) and the whole payment goes to the Release (`release_license.move:94-95`). Reachable under `floor(0)` or a tiny floor, most easily on a long release with small shares. Probe: 3-track release split 3,334/3,333/3,333 BPS, payment 2 → no event, Record stores price 2; payment 10 → `3/3/3` and remainder 1. The Release admin recovers the remainder with `redeem_and_forward` (which divides it by the same split again).

**RS-04 · Info · Accepted — `fixed(0)` and `floor(0)` let any address take Records up to `max_supply` for gas.** No per-buyer limit (`shop.move:60-62`). Documented in SECURITY.md.

**RS-05 · Info · Accepted — Each purchase performs one `send_funds` per track with a nonzero share.** A Release holds at most 255 tracks (`u8::MAX`, `release.move:59`), so a sale makes at most 255 accumulator writes; PTBs that batch many such purchases may hit per-transaction limits and should keep batches small.

**RS-06 · Info · Accepted — No sale event of its own.** Sales are indexed from `RecordPurchasedEvent<Witness, Currency>` and `ReleaseRevenueForwardedEvent<Currency>` (ARCHITECTURE.md), whose `release_id`, split `hash`, per-track `values` and `remainder` suffice to attribute a sale; the event is skipped when no track is paid (RS-03). The remainder dust at the Release needs the Release admin and the `AccumulatorRoot` to redeem.

**RS-07 · Info · Accepted — The Release admin can replace the split after a Pressing is priced.** `set_split` overwrites the shares and hash at any time (`release_license.move:131-134`) and `purchase` reads the split at sale time, so later sales divide per the new split while the buyer's price is unchanged. Recordings that authorized the old hash keep that authorization, which `forward` ignores. The split cannot be removed and its length is fixed by the immutable tracklist. Documented in SECURITY.md; custody of `ReleaseAdminCap` is the control.

## Checked, no finding

- **Payment validation and conservation.** `paid` is read once (`shop.move:59`); `Fixed` requires equality, `Floor` requires `>=` (`shop.move:60-62`), so under- and overpayment on `Fixed` and underpayment on `Floor` abort with `EWrongPayment`. The full `Balance` is forwarded (`shop.move:65`): `set_split` requires one share per track and a total of exactly 10,000 BPS (`release_license.move:125-127`), so the rounded-down track values never exceed `paid` and `Σ values + remainder = paid`; a zero payment forwards nothing and a zero remainder is `destroy_zero`'d (`release_license.move:72,94`). `apply` widens to `u128` (bps), so nothing overflows, and the package divides by nothing. Tests: `complete_sale_delivers_record_and_forwards_proceeds_to_the_tracks` (6,667/3,333 BPS, price 25 → 16/8, remainder 1), `fixed_pricing_rejects_underpayment/overpayment`, `floor_pricing_rejects_underpayment`, `floor_overpayments_are_forwarded_in_full`, `free_pricing_*`, `pay_what_you_want_accepts_nothing_or_more`, `fixed_pricing_accepts_the_maximum_price`.
- **Repricing races.** The signed payment amount cannot be made to pay more; a changed `Fixed` price or a raised `Floor` aborts. Tests: `repricing_up/down_rejects_the_signed_payment`, `floor_repriced_down_keeps_the_overpayment`.
- **Currency binding.** The pricing is keyed by the `Currency` type of the `Balance` (`shop.move:60`), so a pricing in one currency cannot be satisfied with another; an unpriced currency aborts in `sui::dynamic_field` (`EFieldDoesNotExist`). Test: `purchase_rejects_a_currency_not_on_sale`, `two_currencies_are_priced_separately_and_share_one_pressing_sequence`.
- **Release binding.** `object::id(release) == pressing.release_id()` (`shop.move:58`) ties the forwarding tracklist and split to the Pressing's Release; `Release.tracks` (read through `tracks()`) has no setter, and a `key`-only Release is only ever shared by `release::publish` (`release.move:95-101`), so any Release a buyer can pass is published. `set_pricing` makes the same check (`shop.move:99`) so a Pressing is never priced against a foreign Release. Anyone may create a Release of any recordings, but `pressing::new` needs that Release's `ReleaseAdminCap`. Tests: `purchase_rejects_a_different_release`, `set_pricing_rejects_a_different_release`.
- **Split requirement.** `set_pricing` aborts `ENoSplit` unless `release_license::has_split(release)` (`shop.move:100`). No function removes a split: `set_split` only adds or replaces it, and `ReleaseSplitKey` can be constructed only inside `release_license`, so `forward` never reaches its own `ENoSplit` (`release_license.move:194`) for a priced Pressing. `shop::ENoSplit` is 2, `release_license::ENoSplit` is 0. Test: `set_pricing_rejects_a_release_without_a_split`.
- **Authorization and abort order.** `purchase`: `EWrongRelease` (`shop.move:58`) → missing pricing (`EFieldDoesNotExist`, `shop.move:60`) → `EWrongPayment` (`shop.move:61-62`) → `record`'s `EDistributorNotAuthorized` → `EMaxSupplyReached` (`pressing.move:84-85`) → forward (`shop.move:65`). `set_pricing`: `EWrongRelease` → `ENoSplit` → `record`'s `EUnauthorized` from `uid_mut` (`shop.move:99-101`), so a foreign cap with a wrong Release aborts `EWrongRelease`. The witness is created after all shop checks and consumed by `press` (`shop.move:64`). Revocation stops sales immediately. Tests: `unauthorized_record_shop_cannot_press`, `revoked_record_shop_cannot_press`, `maximum_supply_boundary_rejects_the_next_purchase`, `currencies_share_the_same_supply_cap`.
- **Witness privacy.** `Witness` has only `drop`; `new` is `public(package)`; `scripts/check-witness-privacy.sh` confirms both fixtures fail with the expected diagnostics. The remaining exposure is a package upgrade, hence the immutability requirement below.
- **Dynamic-field namespace.** `PricingKey<Currency>` cannot be constructed outside `shop` (probe), and `df::hash_type_and_key` includes the key type, so other Pressing extensions sharing `uid_mut` cannot read-modify or collide with pricings. Admin paths: cap-gated by `record` (`set_pricing_rejects_a_foreign_*`, `remove_pricing_rejects_a_foreign_*`).
- **Events.** `PricingSetEvent` carries the full enum (41 bytes) after the field is written (`shop.move:107`); `Fixed(x)` vs `Floor(x)` is a change; no-ops emit nothing (`shop.move:104`, `pricing_events_capture_only_real_changes`). `PricingRemovedEvent` 32 bytes (`shop.move:114`).
- **Object lifecycle.** The package creates no objects of its own; `Pricing` is `copy + drop + store` and lives only as a dynamic field; `remove_pricing` deletes it. The Record is returned, never transferred by this package.
- **Static linkage.** Calls go to the exact `release_license` (framework `54c88cd`, with `royalty_pool` and `hikida` beneath it), `record` and `miso_music` versions in `Move.lock`; later upgrades of those packages cannot redirect this package's calls.
- **README and SECURITY claims** match the code, including the join keys, byte sizes, `EWrongPayment`/`EWrongRelease`/`ENoSplit` semantics, the basis-point split with remainder to the Release, and the dynamic-field abort for unpriced currencies.

## Operational requirements

- Publish, then `package::make_immutable` and confirm the `UpgradeCap` is gone, before any Pressing authorizes `record_shop::witness::Witness`.
- Set the Release's split (`release_license::set_split`) before pricing; `set_pricing` aborts without one.
- Before authorizing the Witness, verify the linked `release_license` derives the live pools' addresses (RS-02), and create the `Currency` pool for every track with a nonzero share before pricing in that currency (RS-01).
- Keep `PressingAdminCap` and `ReleaseAdminCap` address-owned or in trusted custody; never share or freeze them. The latter controls the split (RS-07).
- PTBs must consume the returned Record, normally with `transfer::public_transfer`.
- Frontends must present `Floor` overpayment as non-refundable and free pricings as unlimited per buyer.
